# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal

develOS is a community for people interested in operating-system development.

We want discussions and collaboration to remain respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful to other contributors.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Discuss technical disagreements without personal attacks.
- Help keep discussions productive.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Threats
- Discrimination
- Deliberate disruption
- Sharing private information without permission

## Moderation

Maintainers may take appropriate action when this code of conduct is violated.

The goal is to maintain a healthy and productive community.
